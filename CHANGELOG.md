# Changelog

## Unreleased

### outline
- **Generated outline (roadmap #407, combines #15, #102 and #46).** The page panel's outline mode now merges four sources into one nested list ordered by page and position: **bookmarks** (title, colour, category, as before), **PDF slide changes** (each PDF file, and each slide where the PDF page under the notebook page changes), **large handwriting** as headings, and **markers** (questions, homework and the importance markers; gaps to complete are not listed). Tap a row to jump to its page; bookmarks keep their "…" menu; the category filter still filters bookmarks.
- **Headings from handwriting size, no OCR and no AI** (`src/ink/headings.ts`, on the lines of `segmentText`, #255): a line is a heading when its letters are at least 1.7 times the page's usual letter height (weighted median of its lines; the notebook's usual height on a page with fewer than three lines), it has two or more strokes, it starts a block (nothing written just above it) and starts near the left of the text. Letters 2.4 times the usual or more make a top-level heading, the others a second level. The outline shows a picture of the ink (nothing is read, so there is no text), cached per heading.
- **Pure builder** `src/model/outline-builder.ts` (no 'obsidian'): the text layout of a page is cached by the identity of its strokes, so an unchanged page costs a pointer comparison per stroke. Nesting follows a level stack (PDF and bookmark, slide, heading, marker). Nothing is stored: **no change to the notebook format** (still v11).
- Settings → *Pages* → *Outline* (a group, open when a source is off): a switch for each source (bookmarks, slide changes, large handwriting, markers). Italian translation included.
- The outline rows are restyled with the design tokens (icon squares, nesting guides, 44 px rows, "you are here" on one row) under `.nb-ui`; rows wrap to two lines in the narrow panel.
- Tests: `tests/outline-builder.test.ts`; harness scenarios `outline-{light,dark}-{tablet,phone}` (`dev/harness/outline-check.ts`).

## 0.12.3 — 2026-10-30

Fixes for what 0.12.2 showed on the tablet (Obsidian for Android, Italian, a vault with no notebooks): an empty creation dialog, a Home with only its title and search pill, an empty *Tag* page, a rail **+** sitting on the brand, and navy panels in the settings. **Not verified on the device**: checked in unit tests and in the browser harness only. Manual re-check: steps 485-492 in `docs/testing.md`. No change to the notebook format (still v11).

### Fixed
- **Root cause, found by audit and reproduced in the harness (likely, not confirmed on the device): every generated picture threw, and took the surface around it down.** The art engine made its canvas with `activeDocument.createEl("canvas")`. Obsidian's `createEl` on a node *appends the new element to that node*; on a Document that means a second root element, and the browser throws `HierarchyRequestError: Only one element on document allowed`. The harness's stand-in for Obsidian made `Document.createEl` a detached create, so it never failed there. With the stand-in made faithful, the harness shows exactly the tablet's screenshots: the header band's art is drawn before *+ Nuovo* and the bell, so the header stopped after the title and the search pill and Home / the *Tag* page drew nothing below it; the creation dialog's art band is drawn before its tabs, so it stayed an empty bar with Obsidian's ×. The rail (no art) and the settings tab were fine. Every detached element is now made with the global `createEl` (the art engine, thumbnails and paper previews, scan, images, PDF pages, excerpts, math, and the canvas that measures text boxes in `src/ink/renderer.ts`: one token, no change to how strokes are drawn). If the device still shows the problem, the error cards below name the real cause.
- **Decoration can no longer break a layout.** The art engine guards itself (`artImage`, `coverImage`, `emptyStateImage`, `folderIconSvg`): a failure is logged once, the caller gets no picture, and after three failures the engine stops trying for the session. The components guard their decoration again (`decorate()` in `ui/components/dom.ts`): subject cards fall back to a plain folder icon, the creation dialog's cover to its plain colour, paper tiles to their label. Home's header art is drawn after its controls.
- **Failures are visible.** Each part of Home, of the library dashboard and of the library view (rail, header, content) and the creation dialog (as a whole, and its preview and form separately) is drawn on its own; one that throws shows a small error card in its place, *Impossibile mostrare …: <message>* with the first stack frame, in the style of the settings tab's error, and the rest is still drawn.
- **No + in the rail.** The compact **+** next to the brand is removed (it sat on *Quaderni / per Obsidian*). Creation is the header's *+ Nuovo*, aligned in the header row beside the search pill and the bell, and Home's *Azioni rapide*, as in `home.png`.
- **Settings look native.** The settings tab drops its navy panel and band backgrounds: Obsidian's own surface with subtle borders (`--background-modifier-border`), Obsidian's text and accent colours, and the search pill without a band behind it (no longer sticky). Section headers, rows, toggles and spacing are unchanged. *Appearance → Style* still styles the plugin's own surfaces.
- Audited and **not** found in `src/`: built-ins newer than Chrome ~105 (`Object.groupBy`, `toSorted`, `Set` methods, `structuredClone`, `Promise.withResolvers`, …), `roundRect`, `ctx.reset` (feature-checked), `letterSpacing`, `checkVisibility`, the popover API, the regex `v` flag; no member of a Modal, ItemView or SettingTab subclass shadows a field of its Obsidian base. esbuild now targets `es2020` / `chrome100` (was `es2021`).

## 0.12.1 — 2026-10-30

Fixes for two regressions of 0.12.0 found in Obsidian for Android: the new Home and the library rendered unstyled, and the plugin's settings tab was gone. **Not verified on the device**: the fixes are checked in unit tests and in the browser harness only, which now loads Obsidian's real button and input rules and runs the whole plugin's start-up. The exact error on the tablet could not be read from here, so part of the cause below is an inference; please report the developer console output if it happens again. Manual re-check: steps 470-477 in `docs/testing.md`. No change to the notebook format (still v11) or to the stroke pipeline.

### Fixed
- **Settings tab missing, and probably the unstyled Home too: one failing start-up step stopped the whole start-up.** `onload` ran some thirty registrations in a row with no guard, and registered the settings tab last. Any exception thrown before that line rejected `onload`. Obsidian then never registered the tab, and very likely never applied the plugin's `styles.css`, while the views registered earlier (Home, the library) still opened from the saved layout. That matches what the tablet showed: a working but completely unstyled Home and no *Notebook* entry under Community plugins. 0.12.0 also rebuilt the settings tab on Obsidian 1.13's declarative API, which Obsidian calls inside `addSettingTab` (to index it for search), and nothing outside Obsidian had ever started the plugin as a whole: the harness built each view on its own. Which step threw on the device is not known: the whole start-up runs cleanly against the harness's stand-in for Obsidian, so the failing call depends on the real app. The fix:
  - Every start-up step is guarded (`main.ts` `step()`): a failure is logged with the step's name, listed in `startupFailures` and reported once in a notice ("some features could not start (…)"), and the other steps still run.
  - The settings tab is registered first.
  - `onunload` copes with services that did not start.
  - The settings tab cannot fail Obsidian's call: `getSettingDefinitions()` never throws. If the definitions cannot be built it returns an empty list, so Obsidian 1.13+ falls back to the hand-drawn tab. Every row's `render` and `visible` callback is guarded, so one broken row cannot take the tab down, and a tab that cannot be drawn shows the error instead of a blank page.
  - An unreadable `data.json` no longer stops the start-up: the plugin runs on the defaults and never writes them over the file in that session (zero data loss).
- **Home, the library and the creation dialog showed Obsidian's grey buttons.** Obsidian's own stylesheet styles every button and text input with selectors of specificity 0,1,1 (`button:not(.clickable-icon)`, `button:hover`, `input[type=search]`: grey fill, shadow, fixed height and padding, no wrapping). These beat the plugin's single-class component rules (0,1,0) in real Obsidian. The harness's stand-in for Obsidian's CSS used a weaker `button` rule, so its screenshots looked right. Every rule of the 0.12 design-system section is now scoped under a new `nb-ui` class, set on the Home/library root and the creation dialog: exactly one class more per rule, so the rules keep their order among themselves. A reset inside the scope neutralises Obsidian's defaults, so component styles always win. The harness's base CSS now contains Obsidian's real button and input rules and the `is-mobile` / `is-phone` / `is-tablet` body classes. With them it reproduces the grey rail items, *Show all*, period dropdown and row menus of 0.12.0, which the new check catches.
- Not a cause: the 0.12.0 release's `styles.css` is byte-identical to the repository's, the stylesheet has balanced braces and no syntax a current Android WebView would drop, and the classes the CSS needs are set by the views themselves, not by the harness.

### Added
- Diagnostics: when Home or the library opens and the plugin's stylesheet is not applied, a notice says so (once per session) and the console logs it.
- Harness: a `doc=plugin` scenario runs the real `NotebookPlugin.onload` against the stub (`dev/harness/plugin-check.ts`). It checks what the plugin registers (settings tab, three views, commands, ribbon) and draws the registered settings tab through both of Obsidian's paths: 1.13+ definitions (the stub now has `update()` and a declarative renderer) and `display()`. It asserts that every section and every row is drawn. It also injects three failures: a start-up step that throws, settings definitions that throw, and an unreadable `data.json`.
- Harness: Home, the library and the creation dialog fail the check when the root is not a flex row, the rail is not a flex column, the subject cards are not on a grid, the tokens do not resolve, or any button shows Obsidian's default button look.
- `tests/styles.test.ts` fails on any design-system rule that is not scoped under `.nb-ui`.
- `npm run harness:check` starts Chrome with `--no-sandbox` when it runs as root (containers, CI).

A bare `contextmenu` at most 150 ms before or 350 ms after the pen's pointerdown (new settings `penButtonContactBeforeMs`, `penButtonContactAfterMs`, in *Pen & input → Advanced*) makes the whole contact the eraser: when it comes after the landing, the live ink of the contact is dropped (nothing committed) and the samples so far are replayed as the eraser; when it comes only after the pen up of a short flick (within the after-window of its landing), the flick's stroke is undone (one undo step) and replayed as the eraser. Later in the stroke, the ink so far is committed and the rest of the contact erases, never touching that kept ink (in *Stroke* mode it used to delete the stroke just written, in *Segment* mode to cut its tail; the same for `buttons & 2` and the chord event); the pen up ends it. With a finger or palm on the page, or lifted less than 600 ms before, a `contextmenu` during a pen stroke changes nothing. The event is always cancelled. A hover click now waits for the before-window. The stroke pipeline is unchanged: only which tool the contact uses.
  - *Pen button test*: notes the order and the gaps between the pen's pointerdown, the `contextmenu` and the first pointermove (`contextmenu Δ=+42 ms after pen down`), and shows a verdict line (button held at contact, during stroke, while hovering).
  - The side button held while the nib touches: a pen landing with `buttons & 2` or `button` 2 and pressure above 0 now starts the button's tool (the eraser by default) instead of opening the radial menu; with pressure 0 (a press while hovering) it still opens the menu. `buttons & 2` / `button` 2 or `buttons & 32` / `button` 5 appearing on a move of an ordinary stroke keep the ink so far and make the rest of the contact the eraser (or the button's tool). The stroke pipeline is unchanged.

## 0.5.2 — 2026-10-30

Hotfix for 0.5.1. Not verified on Android hardware (see `docs/testing.md`). What the S Pen really sends was observed by the user on a Samsung tablet with the Pen button test and Chrome's pen-tracker page; the chorded press during contact (item 3 of *Fixed*) is a hypothesis from one observation and may not hold.

### Fixed
- **The radial menu could not be reached on a Samsung tablet.** With Air command off, the Pen button test saw no event at all when the S Pen side button was pressed *on the page it was tested in*, while Chrome on the same tablet shows the button as a primary press with zero pressure. The menu is now reachable in four ways:
  1. **Side button pressed while the pen hovers** (Samsung / Chromium): `pointerdown` with `button 0`, `buttons 1` and `pressure` exactly 0 (a real nib contact has pressure above 0), also `buttons` going 0 to 1 in a pen `pointermove` / `pointerrawupdate` with pressure 0. It only counts for a pointer that was hovering just before (`buttons 0` seen for the same `pointerId`), never draws and never erases from the pressure-0 phase, and can be switched off (*A zero-pressure press is the side button*). Press the button *before* touching the page: pressed after the nib is down, `buttons` is already 1 and it cannot be told apart.
  2. **Hold the pen still** on the page (see *Changed*).
  3. **Side button pressed while the nib touches** (experimental, setting *Side button pressed while writing*): a dispatched pen `pointermove` / `pointerrawupdate` with `button 0` (instead of -1) while `buttons` stays 1 finishes the stroke so far (kept, never discarded) and the contact then acts as the button's tool until the next such event or the lift. Never from `pointerdown`, never from coalesced or predicted events, only after an ordinary move was seen in the contact, not within 40 ms of the landing, debounced 120 ms; a device whose moves all carry `button 0` never triggers it.
  4. **Finger long-press** (see *Added*).
- The side-button signals that other devices may deliver (`buttons & 2`, `contextmenu`, `auxclick`, a mouse right button after pen activity) are kept.
- Honest texts (English and Italian): the *Pen side button* setting says the button works on Samsung only with the pen hovering, that it may never reach Obsidian on some devices, and names the two alternatives; the Pen button test shows *No side-button signal received yet on this device* until one arrives.

### Changed
- **Pen side button** default is now **Radial menu on click, erase while held**: with the pen hovering, press and release the button to open the menu on the release (at the pen position); press the button and then touch the page to erase (current eraser mode, size and filter) for as long as the contact lasts in that button session; no menu then. New options: *Lasso while held* and *Previous tool while held* (the others: radial menu only, erase while held, nothing). The setting is stored under a new key (`penSideButton`): the 0.5.x default "radial" is not carried over, an explicit "erase while held" or "nothing" is.
- **Open the radial menu by holding the pen still** is more robust and **on by default only while the device has never delivered a side-button signal** (the app remembers the first signal in the settings: `penButtonSeen`). Once one has arrived the default is off; an explicit choice always wins. The pen must be still from the very first contact: 700 ms (was 600 ms) within 6 px of where it landed. Once the stroke has moved it can never open the menu, however long it then rests, so dots, slow strokes and pauses in a stroke are safe. The discarded stroke leaves no dot and nothing for undo, and the pen is not left "down" for palm rejection afterwards. Never for the eraser end; needs the side button action to open the radial menu.
- The Pen button test shows the event type and the pointer id on every line, labels the pressure-0 press (*side button (pressure 0 primary press)*), the touch while it is held, the lift and the release (with *click: opens the menu*), and `pointermove` with `button 0` during contact as *possible side button*. A pressure-0 press counts as a side-button signal and turns the hold-still default off.

### Added
- **Finger long-press opens the radial menu** at the finger (setting *Open the radial menu with a finger long-press*, on by default): a finger still for 500 ms within 10 px, only while finger drawing is off (pen-only), only a single finger, and not when the palm-rejection window says a pen is near. The ring opens beside the finger, not under it, so the fingertip does not cover the sectors. A pan, a pinch or a two-finger tap that starts before 500 ms is not affected.

## 0.4.1 — 2026-10-29

Hotfix for 0.4.0. Not yet verified on Android hardware.

### Fixed
- **Black page, nothing could be written** (reported on a Samsung tablet with S Pen, dark theme): the page area stayed empty — black in the dark theme — and no tool drew, while the toolbar and the page panel worked. Cause: two merges in the 0.4.0 cycle damaged `styles.css` (the "view wave 2" and "integration P1" sections lost their closing braces and many declarations). Browsers with CSS nesting read the rest of the file as nested rules that never match, so the page container was no longer a flex box: the drawing area had zero height, the canvases were never sized and no pointer event reached them. The two sections are restored from their original commits (embed previews and the page panel's hidden/narrow states were affected too).
- One bad stroke, image, PDF background or text box can no longer stop the page from being drawn: every part of a frame is drawn in its own guard, a failure is skipped and logged once in the console (`[notebook] render …`). If painting wet ink fails, the stroke being written is shown on the regular live layer instead, so the pen always leaves ink. Pointer events are guarded the same way (`[notebook] input …`) and a stroke is always closed when the pen lifts.
- Insert image and Insert image page: HEIF/HEIC photos get the same clear message instead of a generic failure (and are not copied into the vault); sizes are read through the scanner's decoder with its fallbacks.
- Scanner: photos in HEIF/HEIC (Samsung "High efficiency pictures") get a clear message instead of "Could not read this photo"; robust decoding (fallbacks, safe canvas sizes for 12-50 MP photos); in-app camera that always yields JPEG.

### Changed
- New setting **Low-latency ink (experimental)**, **off by default**: the pen's wet-ink canvas asks for a low-latency (`desynchronized`) context and `pointerrawupdate` is listened to only when it is on. On some Android GPUs a low-latency canvas is composited as an opaque layer; off, ink is still painted as each pen event arrives, on a regular canvas, with `pointermove` and coalesced samples. If the low-latency context cannot be created, a regular one is used.

### Development
- Browser harness (`dev/harness/`, `npm run harness`, `npm run harness:check`): the real notebook view on a stand-in for the `obsidian` module, checked headlessly in Chrome on old (v3) and new notebooks, light and dark theme, tablet, phone and desktop viewports with their pixel ratios, synthetic pen input and injected faults, with screenshots and composited-pixel checks. - **File format v4 and v5.** v4 adds notebook `meta`, `assets`, page backgrounds (PDF page or image), text and image elements, infinite pages and the `shape` tool. v5 adds the per-stroke `render` field (1 = the old perfect-freehand look, kept for every existing stroke; 2 = the new ink engine). v3 and v4 files migrate in memory and are rewritten as v5 on the first edit. **Files written by 0.4.0 open read-only in older versions** (with a backup copy).
- Stabilization is an adaptive filter (1€ filter): less jitter at rest and much less lag while writing at the same level (default 2).
- Pressure, real or simulated, is decided at the start of each stroke and never switches in the middle of it.
- *Predicted points* is replaced by *Predicted ink*, **off by default** (predicted samples overshoot at turns and at the end of a stroke).

- Duplicating a notebook gives it a new document id.

### Fixed

Found by independent reviews of the new data-safety code:
- The journal is keyed per file identity and per session, so two notebooks or two sessions cannot replay each other's entries; an entry that does not match the file on disk is never applied, a copy goes to the backup folder first.
- Recovery works again after a notebook was unreadable and then became readable.
- Assets are verified before a page refers to them.
- Duplicate page ids are rejected on read and on write.
- Restoring a version aborts if the save of the open notebook fails, or if the version belongs to a different notebook.
