# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), published under the [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) licence. Please read it. In short:

- Be kind and respectful, and assume good faith.
- Welcome people of every background and level of experience.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, insults, trolling or discriminatory language.
- Do not publish other people's private information.

## Scope

This applies to every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and any place where you represent the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@Plagemes](https://github.com/Plagemes), through a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/Plagemes/Obsidian-Notebook/security/advisories/new) or the contact details on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled promptly and confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock threads, or block people who do not follow this code, following the [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) of the Covenant.
